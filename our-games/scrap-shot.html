SCRAP SHOT

In Scrap Shot, players will take control of an inmate and his ability to create a hyper-modular gun that can switch components in-game. General gameplay will pan out similarly to indie titles like Nuclear Throne and Enter the Gungeon (two twin-stick, roguelike shooters).

Unique Selling Points:

What sets this game apart from other titles?

  • Unique Gun Component Swapping Mechanic
  • 2D, Handmade pixel art and animation
  • Co-op multiplayer on release

The Story:

During a seemingly average galactic day, the crew manning a prisonship detected multiple subspace anomalies. A handful of spacecraft slip out from behind and open fire. A naval battle ensues, but the prisonship was destroyed in orbit over a scrap planet. All the inmates, prisoners, and crew, fall to the planet’s surface and must now find a way to survive.
